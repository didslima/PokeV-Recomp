# PokeV-Recomp v0.2.0-rc.1

**Release Candidate — Pre-release for testing**

## Highlights

### Features
- **Windows ZIP Auto-Updater (Phase C, #63):** One-click in-app updates on Windows via a custom ZIP32/ZIP64 extractor and external updater helper (`PokeV-Recomp-updater.exe`) with atomic staging, backup, and `--recover` rollback, preserving `save/`, `mods/`, `larecomp.ini`, and `extracted/`.
- **Ultrawide Cutscene Aspect Preservation (#67):** Pre-rendered cutscenes now preserve their native aspect ratio centered with opaque black pillarboxes/letterboxes on 32:9, 21:9, and 16:10 displays while gameplay remains full-width (`LARECOMP_WIDE_MOVIE_BOX=0` disables).

### Bug Fixes & Performance
- **Kernel wait spin optimization (#58):** Short spin before sleeping in `klock()` and `block_current()` to reduce Poké Ball throw pauses.
- **Poké Ball ground depth clamp (#57):** Depth clamp enabled in color passes with depth, fixing black ground under Poké Balls.
- **Storage snapshots (#60):** Skips storage snapshot copies for shaders that write to global memory, fixing neutral-pose character faces.
- **60 FPS timeline clips (#59, #65):** Parity-aware handling of one-frame timeline clips at 60 FPS, fixing missing ground/grass in wild catches and Tera Raid catch camera motion.
- **60 FPS indoor camera (#61):** Scales indoor camera steps to match 30 FPS smoothness without character shaking.
- **DLAA Tera Raid boss lines (#66):** Tracks object motion constant buffers per `(mesh, program, draw_index)` and guards partial `realloc()` failures.

## Upgrade & Recompilation Notice

- **`GAMELIB_ID` changed:** Updating from `v0.1.1` will trigger a one-time **incremental game-code recompilation** on first launch (`tools/sv_patches.py` updated for 60 FPS fixes).
- **`SHADER_TRANSLATOR_ID` unchanged:** Existing shader caches remain compatible.
- **Saves, settings, and mods** are preserved and remain compatible.

## Known Issues

- **Character profile photograph distortion:** On the Uva Academy Enrollment Application screen (early game), the character profile photo appears squashed/distorted (pre-existing issue, unrelated to #67).
