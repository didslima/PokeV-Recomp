# PokeV-Recomp v0.2.0-rc.2

**Release Candidate — Pre-release for testing**

## Highlights

### Ultrawide Bink Cinematic Fix (#69)
- **Planar Bink video detection with depth testing:** Recognized planar Y/Cb/Cr Bink draws that execute with `depth_test == true` (such as the Miraidon intro cinematic) so they are properly boxed instead of stretched to 32:9.
- **16:9 centering on ultrawide displays:** Preserves native 16:9 aspect ratio centered (e.g., `2560×1440` on `5120×1440`, `2560×1440` on `3440×1440`) without affecting 3D projection or normal gameplay.
- **Opaque black pillarbox bars:** Enforces solid black sidebars at final presentation (`movie_boxed_present_scissor()` + frame-level `movie_boxed_frame` tracking in `vk_present_draw()`) so underlying scene/composite passes never bleed into the side regions.
- **Normal gameplay preserved:** Ordinary gameplay remains full-width (`LARECOMP_WIDE_MOVIE_BOX=0` disables movie boxing).

## Upgrade & Recompilation Notice

- **`GAMELIB_ID` unchanged from `v0.2.0-rc.1`** (`86c55c7268f8418d`): No game-code recompilation needed when upgrading from `v0.2.0-rc.1`.
- **`SHADER_TRANSLATOR_ID` unchanged:** Existing shader caches remain compatible.
- **Saves, settings, and mods** are preserved and remain compatible.

## Known Issues

- **Character profile photograph distortion:** On the Uva Academy Enrollment Application screen (early game), the character profile photo appears squashed/distorted (pre-existing issue).
- **Rendered cutscene transitions:** Brief squashed/duplicated/translucent transition frames between in-engine rendered cutscenes are currently under investigation.
